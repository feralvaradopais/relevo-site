# Relevo Systems Atlas — approved visual language
Date: 2026-11-27. Fernando approved Generative Forms v3 and Operational Architectures v2 in the creative review. These supersede earlier illustration studies. Brand Book v1.1 replaces slides 09 and 10. Approval is for visual direction, not final production assets.

## Series A — Generative Forms v3
Simple, illustrative, airy forms, matching the approved homepage capability cards. One recognizable gesture per figure; fine muted green contours on warm ivory, controlled line density, generous negative space, no technical labels inside the form. Avoid complex spirographs, dense orbital tangles, axes, excessive annotation or decorative complexity. Hero orbital artwork is a distinct larger composition, not the default card illustration.
- A01 Looping Flow — Intelligent Automation: two unequal lobes connected by a fluid narrow waist.
- A02 Layered Surfaces — Custom Apps: three offset rounded rectangular surfaces, each with repeated fine contours.
- A03 Radial Arc — Connected Systems: open fan of gently curved lines around an off-center void.
- A04 Cylindrical Overlap — Operational Intelligence: elongated overlapping elliptical contours with a clear central opening.
- A05 Wave Field — optional flowing contour study.
- A06 Integration Knot — optional restrained interlocking loop study.
First four are canonical capability mappings. Preserve approved silhouettes rather than increasing complexity.

## Series B — Operational Architectures v2
Approved technical editorial diagrams influenced by Georges Calame's wayfinding principles (reference, not copied artwork). Isometric structures, layers, numbered routes, semantic colors, precise arrows, meaningful annotation and strong hierarchy. Paper or dark forest depending on context; dark backgrounds are editorial accents, not the default website surface.
- B01 Operating System — exploded layered structure.
- B02 Process Flow — architectural route through modules.
- B03 Decision Architecture — input, context, rules and branching outcomes.
- B04 Integration Hub — connected systems, data, apps and channels.
- B05 Operational Intelligence — contour surface with data, analysis, decision layers.
- B06 Solution Blueprint — numbered progression across modular spaces.
Use diagrams to communicate real logic; validate direction, layer labels, branches, permissions, exceptions and human oversight against actual system requirements. Boards are conceptual, not verified architectures or deployed products.

## Shared implementation rules
Use original reproducible SVG geometry, parameterized by count, spacing, curvature, phase and seed where useful. Static first; animation optional, meaningful, reduced-motion compatible. Preserve legibility, accessible text alternatives and semantic distinction. Do not rely on hue alone. Do not use raster mockups as production assets. No completed vector library claimed.
Brand decisions: evolve the original two-ring identity (no final logo approved); ivory base with editorial dark moments; compare premium and open-source type before selection. Next: Logo & Typography Lab, then production SVG library and responsive prototype.
