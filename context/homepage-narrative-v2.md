## Thesis
“Construir software nunca ha sido tan accesible. Construir el software correcto sigue siendo un desafío.”
Access to AI coding, low-code and application-building tools does not ensure that businesses have the time, capacity, product judgment, integration or maintenance capability to build reliable systems. Relevo can complement existing technical teams or provide specialized execution where capacity is missing. Do not imply that every company lacks competence or that custom apps always beat existing software.

## Seven-section homepage
1. HERO — AI-FIRST MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS. “Una nueva forma de operar tu empresa.” Body: “Diseñamos y construimos sistemas inteligentes que transforman cómo trabajan las empresas.” Primary CTA: “Exploremos tu operación”. Preserve the preferred visual reference: editorial split composition, forest on paper, two-ring-inspired orbital illustration, annotated Input/Context/Intelligence/Action with human oversight. Logo geometry and typography exploratory.
2. PROBLEM — “Construir software nunca ha sido tan accesible. Construir el software correcto sigue siendo un desafío.” Explain time, capacity, product design, integration and ongoing ownership. Recognizable pains: repetitive work, fragmented information, delayed decisions and limited capacity. No unsupported claims.
3. VISION — “No necesitas convertirte en una empresa tecnológica para operar como una.” Explain how existing systems, people and data connect to intelligence, controlled workflows and action. Human review and authorization where appropriate.
4. SOLUTIONS — two complementary dimensions: capabilities (how we build) and business domains (which problems we solve). Four capabilities: Intelligent Automation — agentes y workflows para ejecutar procesos y reducir trabajo manual; Custom Apps — aplicaciones internas diseñadas alrededor de cómo trabaja tu empresa; Connected Systems — integración de herramientas, datos y procesos; Operational Intelligence — información convertida en decisiones y acciones. Avoid “software a medida” as a public service heading. Domains: Commercial Operations (catalog, quotes, prospecting), Business Operations (documents, approvals), People & Workforce (shifts, requests, hiring), Finance & Administration (reconciliation, expenses). These are example problem spaces, not launched products. Custom Apps can support inventory, internal portals, approvals, dashboards and spreadsheet-bound processes; prefer existing tools when fit is better. Working headline: “Tu empresa es única. Sus herramientas también pueden serlo.”
5. SYSTEMS LAB — “Así se ve una operación más inteligente.” Replace case-study and customer-logo sections entirely. Candidate demos: Catalog Intelligence (inspired by documented proposal, use synthetic data), Document Operations, and an operational Custom App. Distinguish concept / interactive prototype / tested demo / deployed system; only show what exists and works, and clearly label limitations. Do not publish MixGreen identity or prospect information without explicit consent. No claims of verified outcome or client deployment. Lab can support outbound and content experiments; no committed publishing cadence.
6. METHOD — “Desde el problema hasta el sistema funcionando.” Entendemos → Diseñamos → Construimos → Evolucionamos. Establish baseline, scope, permissions, integration, validation and ongoing ownership; agree on maintenance as needed.
7. CONVERSION — “¿Qué parte de tu operación podría funcionar mejor?” Free-text problem plus optional domain. Direct accessible contact fallback. Proposed discovery assistant asks a few questions and offers a cautious hypothesis, then requests consent for contact. No claim of a functioning backend until built.
