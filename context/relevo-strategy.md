# Relevo Studio — website strategic context

This file is a website-specific strategic brief derived from the stable Relevo Studio context in `feralvaradopais/relevo-studio-os`.

It is intentionally concise. `relevo-studio-os` remains the strategic source of truth; this file gives website implementation agents enough approved context to make content and narrative decisions without inventing positioning.

## What Relevo Studio is

Relevo Studio is an AI-native systems studio.

It designs and builds intelligent operational systems for modern organizations that need to sell, operate and make decisions with more clarity, speed and intelligence.

Relevo focuses on practical systems that connect people, tools, data, workflows and customer conversations.

Core idea:

> Relevo designs intelligent systems for organizations in motion.

## What Relevo Studio is not

The website must not position Relevo as:

- a traditional consultancy;
- a software factory;
- a chatbot agency;
- a generic AI automation shop;
- an AI hype brand;
- a content agency.

The distinction matters. The site should communicate that Relevo designs operational systems around real business problems rather than selling isolated technology, generic automation or AI novelty.

## Strategic direction

Relevo Studio should present itself as a premium AI-native systems studio focused on practical operational systems.

The website should therefore favor:

- operational problems and outcomes over AI features;
- systems thinking over isolated tools;
- practical evidence over hype;
- clarity and specificity over abstract technology language;
- the connection between people, workflows, data and software over standalone agents or chatbots.

## Current product / business context

Stable Relevo context currently includes:

### Relevo Outreach

A revenue engagement system for B2B companies.

It helps companies generate qualified conversations and book more meetings through automated prospecting, personalized outreach and intelligent follow-up.

Simple positioning:

> Relevo Outreach helps companies turn prospecting into qualified meetings.

### Serchi

A hiring decision system.

It helps teams organize candidates, CVs, interviews, evaluations, feedback and hiring evidence in one place so they can make better hiring decisions.

Simple positioning:

> Serchi helps teams hire with evidence, not intuition.

### Cauvia

The WhatsApp-first customer-operations thesis originally developed under the working name `Relevo Engage` has evolved into the customer-facing brand Cauvia.

Current Cauvia strategy and product truth live in the separate `cauvia-os` repository family. The final legal/corporate relationship between Cauvia and Relevo Studio is not defined by the stable Relevo context, so the website must not invent or imply one without a newer explicit decision.

### Relevo Engage

`Relevo Engage` is historical context only and must not be used as a current customer-facing brand.

## Language guidance

Prefer language grounded in operations and outcomes, including concepts such as:

- systems;
- operations;
- customer operations;
- qualified conversations;
- lead recovery;
- decision evidence;
- operational clarity;
- intelligent workflows.

Avoid language such as:

- AI magic;
- chatbot;
- passive income;
- fully autonomous;
- no-code empire;
- generic automation.

Do not claim autonomy, capabilities, customer results or business relationships that are not explicitly supported by current source-of-truth material.

## Website narrative principles

When shaping site structure or copy, preserve these principles:

1. Lead with the organizational or operational problem, not the underlying model or technology.
2. Explain Relevo through systems and outcomes rather than a catalog of AI features.
3. Make the work feel concrete: workflows, decisions, conversations, tools, data and operational actions.
4. Keep the brand premium and technically credible; avoid futurist AI clichés.
5. Use the products and systems as evidence of the studio thesis when appropriate, but do not force a portfolio architecture that has not been explicitly approved.
6. Treat visual spectacle and motion as a way to reinforce the systems narrative, not as a substitute for a clear value proposition.

## What is not yet decided

This brief does **not** define the final website strategy. The following remain open until explicitly approved:

- primary website audience;
- primary CTA;
- final homepage information architecture;
- exact service/package taxonomy;
- which products should appear on the public site and how prominently;
- customer proof and case studies;
- pricing or commercial packaging;
- final relationship between Cauvia and Relevo Studio on the public site;
- final copy and messaging hierarchy;
- production framework and motion/rendering architecture.

Agents must surface these as open decisions rather than silently resolving them.
