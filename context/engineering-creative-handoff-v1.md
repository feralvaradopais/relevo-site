## Creative mandate
The approved homepage is an art-direction anchor, not a pixel-perfect spec. Preserve Swiss editorial character, warm paper/forest palette, restrained geometry, selected interlaced isotipo 02 and the two illustration families. Improve typography, composition, proportions, spacing, visual rhythm and responsive experience with intent. Innovate in motion, interaction, navigation and conversion where it improves comprehension or quality. Do not redesign the chosen isotipo. Söhne + Söhne Mono are selected; use Geist/Geist Mono as honest interim open fallback where licensed production fonts are absent. Internal lockup uses Halbfett/Buch as candidate; optical QA remains. Never publish test fonts or use trial-derived wordmarks as production.
A: one simple silhouette per capability (Looping Flow, Layered Surfaces, Radial Arc, Cylindrical Overlap). Keep fine contour spacing and airy massing, not generic repeated ellipses or dense spirographs. Hero may be richer, but subordinate to copy. B: rigorous isometric operational diagrams, layers, routes and annotation; validate semantics. Generate original reproducible vectors in code; tune via browser screenshots at actual size. Do not require a standalone exhaustive SVG library before page work: create only what the page needs and extract reuse when justified. Motion should reinforce meaning, work with reduced motion, and never block text or CTA.

2. Build the page skeleton and hero first; get a coherent visual baseline at 1440 and 390 px. Implement original illustration studies and refine in context, not outside it.

Verify no horizontal overflow or hidden CTA, content legibility, useful image alternatives and real interaction behavior. Record measured results only; flag untested checks. Supply local startup commands, screenshots (desktop/mobile), changed-file summary and design trade-offs. Do not deploy or merge without explicit approval.

Codex independent technical audit can follow. Production go-live requires validated offer, licensed font/brand assets, real contact configuration and privacy/security review.
