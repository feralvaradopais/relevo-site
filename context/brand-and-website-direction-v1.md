# Relevo Studio — Brand & Website Direction v1
Date: 2026-11-27
Status: creative direction endorsed by Fernando for exploration; commercial positioning, copy, visual identity and scope pending joint founder review. This is a new working brief, not an approval to deploy. The earlier cinematic direction is historical per the November 27 OS update; preserve prior assets and decisions.

## Strategic territory
AI-first Management Systems / AI-native Operational Systems. Relevo designs and implements internal software, agents and integrations that improve how businesses operate. Sell measurable outcomes, not a menu of technologies. An AI-first system may combine deterministic workflows, existing ERP/CRM, data, applications, permissions, human review and AI where it adds value. Do not imply replacing ERP, unrestricted autonomy or guaranteed savings. Cauvia explores the external customer journey; brand/legal boundaries remain open.

## Homepage narrative (working Spanish copy)
1. Hero — Eyebrow: AI-FIRST MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS. H1: “Una nueva forma de operar tu empresa.” Supporting copy: “Diseñamos sistemas de gestión, software a medida y agentes de inteligencia artificial que liberan a tu equipo del trabajo manual y transforman tus operaciones.” CTA: “Exploremos tu operación”. Alternative territory: “Construimos sistemas de gestión para la era de la inteligencia artificial.”
2. Problem — “¿Cuánto de tu operación sigue dependiendo de trabajo que podría hacer un sistema?” Four recognizable pains: repetitive work, disconnected systems, late decisions, growth constrained by manual capacity.
3. Vision — “No añadimos inteligencia artificial a tu operación. Diseñamos tu operación para trabajar con inteligencia artificial.” Explain with an accessible architecture diagram: people/objectives/decisions ↔ intelligence and orchestration ↔ applications/workflows/analytics ↔ ERP, CRM, documents, APIs and data. Show authorization, exception handling and human control. This is a conceptual diagram, not an existing platform claim.
4. Solutions — Commercial Operations (catalog quality, search, quotes, prospecting); Business Operations (documents, approvals, routing); People & Workforce (shifts, requests, hiring); Finance & Administration (reconciliation, invoices, expenses). Examples of addressable problems, not four validated products. Do not launch all campaigns simultaneously.
5. Show, don't tell — “Así se ve una operación más inteligente.” Catalog Intelligence concept: detect contradictions and missing attributes, propose corrections, human approval, monitor changes. MixGreen is a documented proposal, not a paid client or live deployment. Use synthetic data or authorized, explicitly attributed proposal evidence; no customer names publicly without consent. Other demos only when concrete and honestly labeled.
6. Method — “Desde el problema hasta el sistema funcionando.” Entendemos (observe real work), Diseñamos (scope, controls, baseline), Construimos (integrate and test), Evolucionamos (measure and iterate). Avoid guarantees.
7. Conversion — “¿Qué parte de tu operación podría funcionar mejor?” Free-text problem, optional area, brief follow-up questions, useful solution hypothesis, then meeting/contact request with consent. Agent is a proposed experience, not a launch dependency: provide functional accessible direct contact fallback. Track qualified conversations, meetings, proposals and paid pilots; never invent ROI.

## Creative direction
Principle: “La complejidad está en lo que construimos. La claridad, en cómo lo mostramos.”
Editorial modernism meets engineering. Swiss-inspired grid, generous whitespace, disciplined typography, organic computational geometry and precise technical diagrams. Warm ivory, ink/forest green, muted sage and restrained accent are exploratory (not final tokens). Use readable contrast, typographic hierarchy and tactile restraint. No generic neon AI gradients, robots, glowing orbs, ornamental network clutter or stock dashboards posing as our product.
Motion should explain cause and effect (inputs → system → actions → outcomes), not become a cinematic prerequisite. Use real or clearly labeled concept UI, semantic HTML, static diagram fallback, reduced-motion support, keyboard navigation and mobile-first legibility.
Review Festa v5 for reusable assets/tokens and typography; retain only what fits. Do not silently overwrite or delete historical assets. No chosen font, logo redesign or final palette yet.

## Three hero exploration briefs (next design step)
A. Editorial systems atlas — large headline, ivory canvas, fine technical linework and a compact system map with annotation. Most direct and clear.
B. Operational intelligence — darker forest canvas, structured modular architecture, carefully staged data-to-action microinteraction and prominent human control. More technical.
C. Product evidence — editorial headline with a tangible catalog/operations interface, before/after workflow and concise labels. Most concrete commercially.
All use the same narrative and CTA; evaluate mobile clarity, perceived trust, buyer comprehension, differentiation, build cost and accessibility before choosing. These are briefs, not generated mockups.

## Website architecture and measurement
Homepage positions the studio; focused landing pages sell a bounded problem (e.g. /solutions/catalog-intelligence). Choose initial buyer/offer with Julio before running campaigns. Use honest evidence, a working CTA, privacy/consent, minimal analytics and tested mobile/performance/reduced motion before release. Do not impose a deadline, budget, final stack or sprint.
