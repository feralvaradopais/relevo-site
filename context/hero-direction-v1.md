# Relevo — Hero Direction v1
Date: 2026-11-27
Status: Editorial Systems Atlas (A) explicitly selected by Fernando as creative direction. Hero below is a working design brief, not final visual identity or approved commercial copy. No production code or visual assets delivered.

## Art direction
Editorial modernism / Swiss grid meets engineering. The object of design is the operational system. Warm paper, typography as protagonist, generous negative space, precise annotations and restrained computational geometry. Avoid neon, robots, glowing orbs, decorative stock dashboards and cinematic dependencies.
Principle: “La complejidad está en lo que construimos. La claridad, en cómo lo mostramos.”

## Hero composition
Desktop reference canvas 1440 px; left narrative, right original systems-atlas illustration, balanced by editorial whitespace. Mobile stacks narrative and CTA before diagram; keep labels legible and avoid horizontal overflow. Header: Relevo wordmark (existing identity to audit), Soluciones, Estudio, contact/navigation. Fine horizontal rule; diagram caption FIG. 01 — INTELLIGENT OPERATIONS.
Working copy:
- Eyebrow: AI-FIRST MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS
- H1: Una nueva forma de operar tu empresa.
- Body: Diseñamos y construimos sistemas inteligentes que transforman cómo trabajan las empresas.
- CTA: Exploremos tu operación.
Test a more explicit body variant mentioning software, agents and integration if buyer comprehension is weak. No guarantees of savings or autonomy.

## Visual language: Relevo Systems Atlas
Build an original reusable graphic grammar: fine linework, modular grids, bounded geometric forms, connection paths, nodes, figure numbering and annotation. Hero study: a central intelligence form with organic orbital linework, surrounded by four labeled stages: 01 Input (personas y procesos), 02 Context (datos y sistemas), 03 Intelligence (análisis y decisiones), 04 Action (ejecución y control). Clarify human oversight; the diagram is conceptual, not a product architecture claim. Create an original set of illustrations using shared rules, initially four motifs:
- Connect: dispersed systems converge.
- Organize: complexity gains structure.
- Intelligence: information becomes actionable decisions.
- Execute: decisions become authorized actions.
Extend only when needed. Do not reproduce references or imply a library already exists.

## Exploratory palette (not production tokens)
Paper #F3F2EB, Mist #E3E7DB, Sage #B0BFA3, Moss #647D5D, Forest #263E30, Ink #19271F. Approximate hierarchy 65/15/8/4/6/2 percent respectively, not a strict ratio. Use dark ink/forest for body text and verify contrast; sage/moss can be accents rather than small text. Audit Festa v5 for continuity before locking tokens.

## Typography
Explore Neue Haas Grotesk, Suisse Int'l, Söhne, ABC Diatype, Geist or Inter for editorial sans; IBM Plex Mono or Geist Mono for technical annotations. No font selected or licensed yet. Validate Spanish glyphs, web licensing, loading/performance and readable mobile hierarchy. Strong display hierarchy, restrained body, uppercase mono annotations in moderation.

## Motion
Meaningful transformation: dispersed elements organize into a coherent system; reveal links and context before actions. Keep CTA and reading independent from animation. Prefer lightweight SVG/CSS or equivalent after feasibility review; provide static fallback and reduced-motion behavior. Avoid autoplay spectacle, scroll hijacking, mandatory WebGL and complex rendering as a launch gate.

## Design principles
Clarity over complexity. Intelligence by design. Systems, not decoration. Motion with meaning. Evidence over promises. Quiet sophistication.
References for study (not assets or affiliation): Josef Müller-Brockmann (grid), Dieter Rams (functional reduction), Manolo Gamboa Naon (generative geometry), Pentagram (systemic identity).
