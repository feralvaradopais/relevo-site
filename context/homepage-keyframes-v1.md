# Relevo Studio — Approved Keyframe Sequence v1

## Status

**Approved visual reference sequence for the next Scene Lab iteration.**

This document refines the six-act homepage storyboard into **nine visual reference states**.

It does **not** create nine cognitive sections. The homepage still has the six approved acts and copy defined in `context/homepage-storyboard-v1.md`.

This document supersedes the earlier six-keyframe planning list in the storyboard. The new sequence was discovered and approved through visual exploration because three intermediate states are necessary to make the film continuous and intelligible:

- `02.5` — the illustrated world has fully become an operational particle sculpture;
- `03.5` — the inspection lens expands and the viewer enters the revealed system;
- `05.5` — the verified system begins to become geography again.

The visual sequence is therefore:

> **WORLD → DISASSEMBLY → OPERATION → INSPECTION → ENTER SYSTEM → BUILD → VERIFY → SYSTEM BECOMES WORLD → RESOLVED WORLD**

The business narrative remains:

> **A company already works. Relevo reveals where work still depends on people bridging gaps, understands the structural cause, builds the missing system, proves it works, and leaves the operation better prepared to move forward.**

---

## 1. Core rule: one film, not nine images

The nine references are not independent illustrations.

They are stills from one continuous reversible film.

Every state must visibly inherit from the state before it through the same:

- WORLD geometry language;
- MATTER / particle vocabulary;
- OPTICS language;
- atmosphere;
- chroma logic;
- lighting logic;
- camera space;
- underlying material.

The coding agent should interpolate between known states rather than invent new art direction between them.

The visual references produced during creative exploration are **art-direction targets**, not production assets and not UI specifications.

Generated labels, captions, warning icons, typography and interface details inside reference images are conceptual annotations only unless explicitly approved elsewhere.

Production DOM copy and UI must follow Festa.

| Ref | Internal name | Homepage role | Visual meaning |

## 3. KF01 — HERO WORLD

### Copy relationship

> **Hacer ligero lo que pesa.**
>
> Construimos sistemas para que tu operación avance con menos fricción y más capacidad.
>
> **Para empresas que ya funcionan y quieren crecer mejor con software, datos e IA.**

### Approved visual direction

A vast original digital valley / canyon at twilight.

The world feels:

- dark;
- premium;
- quiet;
- monumental;
- atmospheric;
- already functioning.

Several ascending aerial sculptural forms live in the sky. They can recall the emotional quality of hot-air-balloon flight, but must remain original, dark and engineered rather than tourism imagery.

The world contains restrained warm routes / lights that imply latent connectivity without already looking like a network diagram.

### Composition

- broad landscape;
- deep foreground / middle / background separation;
- large negative-space territory for DOM copy;
- aerial forms at different depths and heights;
- subtle asymmetry rather than postcard symmetry.

### Motion target

- almost imperceptible push-in;
- slow independent ascent and drift;
- atmospheric haze;
- low particle presence;
- subtle parallax.

### Chroma

Near-black / charcoal with purple atmosphere and restrained warm environmental light.

### Important production note

The hero reference establishes the **master world language** for every later state.

---

## 4. KF02 — WORLD DISASSEMBLY

### Story role

The hero does not disappear. It reveals what it is made of.

This is the visual bridge into Act 02.

### Approved visual direction

The same valley and aerial forms remain recognisable while parts of the scene become particulate.

Target balance:

> approximately **60–70% recognisable world / 30–40% exposed matter** at the strongest reference moment.

Terrain edges become granular, haze becomes particles, illuminated surfaces expose discrete matter and some aerial forms begin releasing particles.

Operational routes become more visible.

### What this is not

- not a destructive explosion;
- not a hard cut;
- not chaos;
- not a new particle scene.

### Production correction from exploration

Any warning / alert icons visible in generated references are **not approved production language**. Friction must later be expressed through particle behavior rather than warning symbols.

---

## 5. KF02.5 — OPERATION SCULPTURE

### Copy relationship

> **Tu empresa funciona.**
>
> **Pero demasiado trabajo todavía depende de alguien.**
>
> Alguien busca.
>
> Alguien responde.
>
> Alguien conecta lo que debería estar conectado.
>
> **Y mientras tanto, una venta, una decisión o un cliente espera.**

### Story role

This is the crucial missing state discovered during visual exploration.

After the world disassembles, particles do not remain as dust. They reorganize into a large suspended **operational sculpture**.

Conceptually:

> **WORLD → MATTER → OPERATION**

The company that looked like geography from outside now reads as a working operation from inside.

### Sculpture language

The sculpture should feel like:

> **topology + current + architecture + organism**

It may contain:

- dense regions;
- cavities;
- routes;
- branching structures;
- transfer points;
- areas with different levels of activity.

It must not become:

- a brain;
- a sphere;
- a random blob;
- a generic neural network;
- a literal process diagram.

### Behavioral friction

The sculpture is functioning, but small behaviors reveal dependence:

- a route waits;
- one transfer takes an unnecessary detour;
- a signal accumulates;
- two functional regions depend on an additional bridging action;
- a transfer only completes after another event intervenes.

The point is not that the company is broken.

The point is:

> **it works because people still complete what the system does not know how to complete.**

### Particle hierarchy

- structural particles define silhouette and topology;
- atmospheric particles establish depth;
- a few brighter transfer particles carry meaningful movement.

### Chroma

Purple dominant; pink only as sparse friction / waiting signal; warm amber can survive as memory of the hero world.

### Production correction from exploration

Any generated labels such as “procesos”, “clientes”, “decisiones”, etc. are explanatory annotations only and must not be baked into WebGL.

---

## 6. KF03 — INSPECTION LENS / STRUCTURAL GAP

### Copy relationship

> **El problema que se ve no siempre es el problema real.**
>
> Por eso no empezamos por la tecnología.
>
> **Primero entendemos qué está frenando el negocio.**

### Story role

We do not replace the operation sculpture. We change our ability to read it.

The camera isolates a region that already behaved suspiciously in KF02.5.

An optical lens reveals a structural cause underneath the visible symptom.

### Lens language

The lens is not a detective prop.

It is perceived through:

- refraction;
- subtle edge / rim;
- focus;
- minimal aberration;
- halo;
- particle response.

### Inside vs. outside

Outside the lens:

- complexity;
- ambiguity;
- multiple competing paths.

Inside the lens:

- secondary information quiets down;
- relevant paths become legible;
- two functional regions become clearly visible;
- the missing relationship appears.

### The gap

The key insight is represented by a small empty region between two parts that should coordinate.

Signals arrive at one side but cannot progress naturally to the other.

No warning icon is required.

No arrow is required.

The visual should create the simple feeling:

> **“Ahí está.”**

### Chroma

Teal dominates the inspected / understood region. Purple remains in the surrounding matter.

### Production correction from exploration

Generated words such as `GAP`, `ORIGEN`, `DESTINO`, `CONEXIÓN AUSENTE` or `DEPENDENCIA HUMANA` are conceptual annotations only. The final scene must communicate the idea primarily through behavior and composition.

---

## 7. KF03.5 — ENTER THE SYSTEM

### Story role

This state bridges discovery and intervention.

The optical lens expands until its edge moves outside the viewport.

What was clear only inside the lens now becomes the full visual world.

Conceptually:

> **we are no longer observing the system; we are inside what we understood.**

### Approved visual direction

- two large structural regions remain separated;
- the former gap remains readable;
- particles from each side begin orienting toward the missing relationship;
- new trajectories appear;
- matter begins preparing to reorganize;
- the solution is not complete yet.

This is the tension immediately before construction.

### Chroma

Teal remains strong, while purple begins returning in the matter that will form the new system.

### Camera

Closer and more immersive than KF03. The lens itself no longer needs to be visible as a circle.

---

## 8. KF04 — BUILD / REORGANIZATION

### Copy relationship

> **El sistema empieza antes que la IA.**
>
> **Conectamos lo que ya existe.  
> Construimos lo que falta.**
>
> Puede ser un agente.  
> Una herramienta.  
> Un flujo.  
> Una capa de datos.

### Story role

This is the main visual climax.

The material now changes behavior from observation to construction.

### Approved visual direction

The two previously separated regions remain visible while a new intermediate architecture grows between them.

Particles, threads and nodes reorganize coherently.

Some routes already begin to cross the former gap while other pieces are still assembling.

The keyframe should capture **the system forming**, not a completely finished system.

### Form language

The new structure can feel like:

- architecture;
- infrastructure;
- living topology;
- engineered current;
- a coherent operational layer.

It must not become:

- a literal bridge;
- a reactor sphere;
- a circuit-board cliché;
- a robot;
- a dashboard;
- four service icons.

### Agent / tool / flow / data layer

These four words may become **intermediate morph states inside Act 04**.

The static reference does not need to show four literal objects simultaneously.

The same matter should be able to shift configuration while remaining recognisably one system.

### Chroma

Purple returns as the dominant identity chroma. Teal supports active connections. Warm highlights remain sparse.

---

## 9. KF05 — VERIFIED SYSTEM

### Copy relationship

> **Construimos lo que el día a día nunca alcanza a construir.**
>
> **Observamos.  
> Construimos.  
> Probamos.**
>
> **Primero lo comprobamos.**
>
> **Después lo dejamos funcionando.**

### Story role

The spectacle drops.

The system built in KF04 stabilises and proves itself through operation.

### Approved visual direction

The same architecture is now calm, coherent and complete.

A few clear signals:

- enter;
- traverse the system;
- cross the formerly missing relationship;
- exit successfully.

Multiple signals can exist at different progress positions in the reference frame to imply repeated successful operation.

The newly built connection should no longer shout for attention. It should feel like a natural part of the whole.

### Emotional target

KF04 says:

> **“This can be built.”**

KF05 says:

> **“It works.”**

The second feeling is quieter and commercially important.

### Chroma

Deep purple dominant; teal routes; restrained warm signal particles; almost no pink.

### Production correction from exploration

Generated labels such as `SEÑALES DE ENTRADA`, `SISTEMA RELEVO FUNCIONANDO`, `SEÑALES DE SALIDA` and `OBSERVAMOS / CONSTRUIMOS / PROBAMOS / FUNCIONA` are explanatory annotations only, not approved WebGL text.

---

## 10. KF05.5 — SYSTEM BECOMES WORLD

### Story role

This is the second crucial intermediate state discovered during exploration.

The verified system does not cut back to the hero landscape.

The camera pulls far away until infrastructure progressively becomes geography.

Conceptually:

> **SYSTEM → OPERATION → WORLD**

### Approved visual direction

At the strongest reference moment, the frame should read roughly as:

> **50% system / 50% landscape.**

The same features change meaning with scale:

- nodes become distant lights;
- active paths become roads / valleys / rivers of light;
- structural particle masses become mountains and terrain;
- atmospheric particles become haze;
- system illumination becomes environmental illumination.

One or more aerial forms may begin to reappear at long distance, but the final world is not fully resolved yet.

### Meaning

The visual idea is:

> **the system is no longer a technology layer sitting on top of the operation; it has become the way the operation works.**

### Production correction from exploration

Any explanatory text embedded in generated reference images is not part of the final scene.

---

## 11. KF06 — RESOLVED WORLD / CONVERSATION

### Copy relationship

> **Hay una parte de tu operación que podría estar funcionando mucho mejor.**
>
> **Encontrémosla.**
>
> Cuéntanos qué está pasando.
>
> **Nosotros hacemos las preguntas.  
> Tú no necesitas tener clara la solución.**

### Story role

Return to the same visual family as KF01, but not the identical world.

The final world is a **resolved version of the opening world**.

### Approved visual direction

The landscape should feel:

- more open;
- lighter;
- calmer;
- more legible;
- better connected;
- more spacious.

The horizon opens.

Routes feel integrated rather than exposed as technical network graphics.

Aerial forms sit higher and farther apart, suggesting lightness and capacity rather than triumph.

The ending is not a corporate “happy world”.

It is:

> **clarity + calm + capacity + possibility.**

### Conversation composition

The environment should be authored with deliberate negative space for the conversion experience.

The conversation becomes the primary interface of the final scene.

It must not feel like a chatbot pasted into the bottom-right corner.

### Production correction from exploration

The generated final reference includes provisional typography and chat UI. Those are **composition references only**.

Production typography, spacing, controls, borders, action colors and behavior must follow Festa.

### Relationship to KF01

KF01:

- mystery;
- weight;
- darker atmosphere;
- lower aerial forms;
- latent routes.

KF06:

- clarity;
- lightness;
- more open horizon;
- higher aerial forms;
- integrated routes;
- conversation-ready negative space.

The viewer should recognise the same world and feel that something changed without seeing a naive before / after.

## 12. Chroma arc

Use chroma narratively, with one dominant chroma per moment.

Approximate direction:

| State | Dominant chroma / feeling |
| --- | --- |
| KF01 | near-black + purple atmosphere / mystery |
| KF02 | purple + sparse pink / friction emerging |
| KF02.5 | purple / functioning operation |
| KF03 | teal / inspection and legibility |
| KF03.5 | teal shifting toward purple / intervention becomes possible |
| KF04 | purple + teal / construction and coordination |
| KF05 | deep purple + restrained teal / stability and proof |
| KF05.5 | purple + warm horizon / system becoming environment |
| KF06 | purple + warm open horizon / possibility |

Pink remains action / signal color, not a general decorative wash.

## 13. What generated references are allowed to contain vs. production

Generated keyframes can contain artifacts that help communicate an idea during review.

They are not automatically approved for implementation.

### Reference-only artifacts

Do not automatically reproduce:

- warning icons;
- labels;
- `GAP` text;
- explanatory captions;
- fake UI;
- generated typography;
- generated navigation;
- pseudo-HUD elements;
- overly literal network diagrams.

### Approved transferable qualities

Do preserve:

- composition;
- scale relationships;
- atmosphere;
- depth;
- world continuity;
- particle density hierarchy;
- focal hierarchy;
- chroma progression;
- lens behavior concept;
- the build / verify contrast;
- the world → matter → operation → system → world arc.

## 15. Implementation gate

The visual direction is now sufficiently defined for the next Scene Lab implementation pass.

Before integrating the full homepage, the Scene Lab should prove:

1. KF01 can transform continuously into KF02;
2. KF02 can resolve into the KF02.5 operation sculpture without a scene cut;
3. the same sculpture can support KF03 optics and the structural gap;
4. the lens can expand into KF03.5 without introducing a new world;
5. KF03.5 can reorganize continuously into KF04;
6. KF04 can settle into the quieter KF05 while preserving geometry continuity;
7. KF05 can pull back through KF05.5 into KF06;
8. all major transitions reverse correctly with scroll;
9. the DOM copy remains legible throughout;
10. mobile and reduced-motion preserve the same story with fewer visual events.

Success is not the number of effects.

Success is that the viewer experiences **one coherent world changing meaning through scale, matter, optics and behavior**.
